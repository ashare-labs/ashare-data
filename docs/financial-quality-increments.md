# Financial quality increments

The common release freezes small unit-definition layers, reviewed daily visibility and a reference-coverage ledger. Native observations and historical releases remain immutable. `FinancialTables` and `data financial-tables` read the release-bound rules by default.

Additional unit layers must bind the immediately previous release, match the audited extraction set, preserve prior rule cutoffs and have disjoint source/statement/field/title/currency/scope keys. Units do not grant historical visibility or canonical metric equivalence.

`reference-coverage` explains captured omissions using exchange-code periods, explicit source suspension flags, verified listing ends or exact same-instrument observations under a source alias. Suspension does not imply a zero or absent statutory limit. `reference-query --family price_limit` can follow only the observation IDs approved in that release ledger.

Reviewed official daily fields use a verified complete exchange-calendar capture when supplied. The first eligible date is the next trading session after the dated announcement. This does not grant an intraday publication time, remove known revision blockers or certify all historical fields.

Small closed candidates may use `financial_release.compose(..., bounded_append=True)`: at most 10,000 physical rows; full validation of the candidate; exact matching of overlapping parent keys and values and composite contexts; inherited verified parent counts. Replacement graphs require full validation. This avoids re-sorting all inherited financial rows for small append-only evidence increments.

2026-10-21 repair scope: three Sina amount rules (OTHERINCO, INTERESTEXPENSE, DEVEEXPE; 319,017 numeric observations); 32,091 old candidate limit gaps explained as 2,419 outside code validity, 1,486 existing alias observations, 28,184 explicit suspended days and two after delisting. Eleven reviewed reports contribute 34 daily fields across five securities, added to seven previous visibility rows. These numbers describe this reviewed scope, not complete market history. Originals and account ledgers are unchanged.

Runtime publication and rollback receipts live under the configured financial-reference/quality-repair-20261021 directory. The formal common-release manifest contains the immutable proof paths; temporary renderings are not query dependencies.
